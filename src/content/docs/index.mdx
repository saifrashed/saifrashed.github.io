Saif Rashed (25) is a z/OS DevOps engineer for the Current Account & Routing team at Rabobank, where he works on mission-critical, highly available financial infrastructure. In 2020, he began his bachelor program in Software Engineering at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences. During his studies, he completed placements at companies such as CERN, AMS-IX, and Rabobank. After graduating cum laude, he started his master’s program at the University of Amsterdam, where he researched the use of artificial intelligence to improve the software engineering process.
